# Head office facilities

## Address and hours

The head office is at Maasboulevard 120, Rotterdam. The building is open from 06:30 to 21:00 on weekdays.

## Desk booking

Desks are booked in the DeskFlow app up to two weeks ahead. Unused bookings are released at 10:00.

## Parking

There are 180 parking spaces, of which 40 have electric vehicle chargers. Parking is booked in DeskFlow.
