# Incident severity levels

## SEV1

SEV1 is a complete outage of a customer-facing system or a confirmed data breach. The on-call engineer must acknowledge within 5 minutes and an incident commander is assigned within 15 minutes. Status updates go out every 30 minutes.

## SEV2

SEV2 is a major degradation affecting more than 10 percent of customers. Acknowledgement within 15 minutes; updates every hour.

## SEV3

SEV3 is a minor issue with a workaround. It is handled in business hours with a target resolution of three working days.

## Post-incident review

Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident needs a blameless post-incident review within five working days.
