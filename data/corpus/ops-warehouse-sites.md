# Warehouse network

## Rotterdam

The Rotterdam distribution centre is the largest site, with 64,000 square metres and 220 dock doors. It operates 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

## Ghent

The Ghent site has 38,000 square metres and specialises in temperature-controlled storage between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius for pharmaceutical customers.

## Antwerp

The Antwerp cross-dock has 12,000 square metres and handles transfers between ocean containers and road trucks.

## Duisburg

The Duisburg rail hub connects to the European rail freight network and handles about 40 block trains per week.
