# Driver hours and rest

## Daily driving

Drivers may drive at most 9 hours per day, extendable to 10 hours twice per week, in line with EU rules.

## Breaks

After 4.5 hours of driving, a break of at least 45 minutes is required. It can be split into 15 minutes followed by 30 minutes.

## Weekly rest

A regular weekly rest period of at least 45 hours is required. Company policy does not allow regular weekly rest to be taken in the truck cab.
