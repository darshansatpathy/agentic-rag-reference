# Forklift and warehouse safety

## Licensing

Only staff with a valid internal forklift licence may operate forklifts. The licence is renewed every three years after a practical test.

## Speed limits

The speed limit for forklifts inside buildings is 6 km/h and 10 km/h in outdoor yards.

## Personal protective equipment

Safety shoes and high-visibility vests are mandatory on all warehouse floors. Hard hats are required in high-bay racking aisles.

## Near misses

Near misses must be reported in the SafeTrack app before the end of the shift.
