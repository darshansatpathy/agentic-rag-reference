# Data retention schedule

## Customer shipment records

Shipment records, proofs of delivery and customs documents are kept for seven years after the end of the financial year.

## CCTV footage

Warehouse CCTV footage is kept for 30 days unless it is needed for an investigation.

## Application logs

Application and access logs are kept for 13 months in the central log platform.

## Recruitment data

Data about unsuccessful job candidates is deleted four weeks after the position is filled, unless the candidate agrees to a longer period of up to one year.
