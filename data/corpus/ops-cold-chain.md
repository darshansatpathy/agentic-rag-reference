# Cold chain handling

## Temperature excursions

If a pharmaceutical shipment's temperature leaves the 2 to 8 degrees Celsius range for more than 30 minutes, it is quarantined and the customer's quality team is notified within two hours.

## Monitoring

Every cold-chain pallet carries a data logger that records temperature every 5 minutes.

## Pre-cooling

Trailers must be pre-cooled to the target temperature for at least 45 minutes before loading.
