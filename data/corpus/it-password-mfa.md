# Passwords and multi-factor authentication

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; they must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA, two-factor) is mandatory for email, VPN and every system that holds customer data. The approved methods are the Authenticator app and FIDO2 security keys. SMS codes are not allowed.

## Password manager

Every employee is given a licence for the company password manager, Vaultline, and must use it for work accounts.
