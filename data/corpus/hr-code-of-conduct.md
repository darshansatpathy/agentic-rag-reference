# Code of conduct

## Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept gifts or hospitality worth up to 75 EUR per supplier per year. Anything above that value must be declared in the gifts register and approved by the compliance officer.

## Speaking up

Concerns about misconduct can be raised anonymously through the SpeakUp hotline, which is run by an independent provider and available 24 hours a day.

## Conflicts of interest

Any personal relationship with a supplier, customer or job candidate must be declared to the line manager before taking part in a decision that involves them.
