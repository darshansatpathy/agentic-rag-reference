# Corporate card rules

## Limits

The standard corporate card limit is 3,000 EUR per month. Directors have a limit of 10,000 EUR per month.

## Lost cards

Report a lost or stolen corporate card to the card provider's 24-hour line immediately and notify finance within one working day.

## Not allowed

Corporate cards must not be used for cash withdrawals, personal purchases or gift cards.
