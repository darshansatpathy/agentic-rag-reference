# New joiner onboarding checklist

## First day

New joiners collect their laptop and badge from the IT service desk on level 2 of the Rotterdam head office. The badge photo is taken on the spot.

## First week

Complete the mandatory security awareness course and the code of conduct training in the Learning portal within five working days of the start date.

## Probation

The probation period is six months for all roles. A probation review meeting is held with the line manager in month five.
