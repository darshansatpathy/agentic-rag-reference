# Customer service level tiers

## Standard

Standard tier customers get next-business-day delivery within the Benelux with 97 percent on-time performance and email support during business hours.

## Priority

Priority tier adds same-day delivery for orders placed before 11:00, 98.5 percent on-time performance and phone support from 07:00 to 22:00.

## Enterprise

Enterprise tier adds a dedicated account manager, 99.2 percent on-time performance, 24/7 phone support and service credits of 5 percent of the monthly fee for each 0.5 percentage point below target.
