# Acceptable use of IT

## Generative AI tools

Only the company's approved AI assistant may be used with internal or customer data. Public AI chatbots must not be given confidential, personal or customer information.

## Personal devices

Personal phones may access email and chat through the managed work profile. Personal laptops must not be used to access internal systems.

## Software installation

Software can be installed only from the Company Portal. Requests for other software go through the IT service desk and a security review that takes up to ten working days.
