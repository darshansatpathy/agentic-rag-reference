# Hybrid and remote work policy

## Eligibility

Office-based roles may work remotely (work from home) up to three days per week after completing probation. Warehouse and fleet roles are not eligible for remote work.

## Home office allowance

Eligible employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 600 EUR for a chair, desk or monitor, claimed through the expenses system within the first six months of becoming eligible.

## Working abroad

Remote work from another country is allowed for at most 20 working days in any rolling 12-month period and requires written approval from HR and the employee's director.
