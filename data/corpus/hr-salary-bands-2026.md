# Salary bands 2026 (confidential)

## Engineering bands

Band E3 ranges from 58,000 to 72,000 EUR. Band E4 ranges from 70,000 to 88,000 EUR. Band E5 (staff engineer) ranges from 86,000 to 110,000 EUR.

## Operations bands

Band O2 (shift supervisor) ranges from 41,000 to 49,000 EUR. Band O3 (site manager) ranges from 55,000 to 68,000 EUR.

## Annual review

The 2026 merit budget is 3.8 percent of base salary across all bands, applied on 1 April.
