# 2027 price list draft (confidential)

## General increase

The draft 2027 general rate increase is 4.5 percent for standard tier and 3.9 percent for enterprise tier contracts.

## Fuel surcharge

The fuel surcharge formula will be indexed weekly instead of monthly from 1 January 2027.
