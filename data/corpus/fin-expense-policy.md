# Travel and expense policy

## Approval thresholds

Expenses up to 500 EUR are approved by the line manager. Expenses between 500 and 5,000 EUR also need the cost-centre owner's approval. Anything above 5,000 EUR needs the finance director's approval.

## Per diem

The daily meal allowance (per diem) is 45 EUR within the Netherlands and Belgium, 60 EUR elsewhere in the EU, and 80 EUR outside the EU.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of six hours or longer. Business class requires director approval.

## Submission deadline

Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense date, with itemised receipts attached.
