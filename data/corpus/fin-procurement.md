# Procurement policy

## Quotes

Purchases from 10,000 EUR need three written quotes. Purchases from 100,000 EUR need a formal tender run by the procurement team.

## Preferred suppliers

Office supplies, travel and IT hardware must be bought from preferred suppliers listed in the SupplierHub catalogue.

## Payment terms

Standard payment terms for suppliers are 45 days from invoice date. Small businesses with fewer than 50 employees are paid within 15 days.
