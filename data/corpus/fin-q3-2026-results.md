# Q3 2026 management accounts (confidential)

## Revenue

Q3 2026 revenue was 184.2 million EUR, up 6.1 percent year on year, driven by contract logistics in the Benelux.

## Margin

Operating margin was 7.4 percent, down from 8.0 percent in Q2 because of higher diesel prices.

## Outlook

Full-year revenue guidance is unchanged at 720 to 740 million EUR.
