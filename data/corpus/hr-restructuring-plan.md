# Warehouse network restructuring plan (confidential)

## Scope

The Antwerp cross-dock will close on 30 September 2027. Its 140 roles will be offered transfers to the Rotterdam and Ghent sites.

## Consultation

Formal consultation with the works council starts on 15 January 2027 and is expected to take 12 weeks.
