# Fleet decarbonisation programme

## Targets

Aurora Freight aims to cut fleet carbon emissions by 50 percent by 2030 compared with 2022, and to reach net zero by 2040.

## Electric trucks

The company runs 85 battery-electric trucks on urban routes in Rotterdam, Ghent and Antwerp, and plans to reach 300 by the end of 2028.

## Biofuel

Long-haul trucks use HVO100 renewable diesel where it is available, which cuts lifecycle emissions by up to 90 percent.
