# Travel security

## Booking

All business travel is booked through the TravelDesk portal so the company can locate travellers in an emergency.

## High-risk destinations

Travel to countries rated high risk by the security team requires a security briefing and approval from the chief operating officer.

## Emergency line

The 24-hour travel emergency line is run by the company's assistance provider and is printed on the back of every employee badge.
