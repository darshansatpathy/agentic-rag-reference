# Damage and loss claims

## Deadlines

Visible damage must be noted on the proof of delivery and claimed within 7 days. Hidden damage must be claimed within 14 days of delivery. Loss claims must be filed within 60 days of the planned delivery date.

## Liability

Liability for road transport follows the CMR convention, limited to 8.33 special drawing rights per kilogram of gross weight unless the customer bought declared-value cover.

## Resolution

The claims team acknowledges a claim within two working days and aims to settle within 30 days.
